Guitar tab you can read, hear and play along with

tabz turns YouTube play-along videos into tab, opens your Guitar Pro files, reads PDFs and pictures of tab into scores you can hear, and plays them along with a backing track while a line moves through the music.

Your whole collection

Every tab you own, in one library

Point tabz at a folder and it brings in Guitar Pro files, PDFs, pictures of tab, tab videos and zips of them. Copies are skipped, and a song saved as .gp, .gpx and PDF comes in once.

  • Browse by artist, genre, acoustic or electric, playing style or difficulty
  • Versions of the same song live in one card
  • Star favorites; Explore suggests what to play next
  • Cover art and genre fill in by themselves

Tab you can hear

PDFs and videos become playable scores

tabz reads the fret numbers in tab from videos, PDFs and pictures into notes, so you can hear the tab and follow it like a Guitar Pro file, or save it as one. The original picture stays as it was.

  • About 9 in 10 notes right on PDFs exported from Guitar Pro
  • Rhythm is a best guess from the spacing
  • Switch between the picture, the playable score and the original pages

Play along

A line moves through the music while you play

Pick what you hear: a backing track, the tab's own sound with your part turned off, just a click, or nothing at all. Slow it down to a quarter speed without the backing track changing pitch.

  • Find a backing track on YouTube or use your own audio
  • Line it up by ear and the sync is saved with the song
  • Click and count-in on top of anything, loops for hard bits

From YouTube

Play-along videos into clean tab

Paste a link or a playlist, or search. tabz finds the tab on screen, follows it as it swaps or scrolls, and keeps every bar as a PDF and as a song that reflows to any window.

  • Works on any channel's style, no per-channel rules
  • Song, artist, capo, tuning, tempo and chords filled in
  • Highlights, playheads and dark skins cleaned to black on white

Getting started

Up and playing in a minute

  1. Download tabz.exe from the releases page and put it in its own folder. It keeps its library next to itself.
  2. Add tabs. Paste a YouTube link, search for a song, or choose a folder of Guitar Pro files and PDFs.
  3. Press play. Pick a backing track, the tab's sound or just the click, set the speed, and play along.
